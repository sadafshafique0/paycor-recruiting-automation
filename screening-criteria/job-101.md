# Warehouse Associate (Job ID 101) — fictional example

We are hiring a warehouse associate to receive, pick, pack and ship orders in a fast-paced
distribution center.

## REQUIRED
- 2+ years of warehouse, logistics or fulfillment experience
- Comfortable with inventory / WMS software

## PREFERRED
- Forklift certification
- Excel

## DISQUALIFIERS
- No relevant experience / internship only
- Located outside the hiring region with no relocation
- Job hopper (multiple <1-year stints)
